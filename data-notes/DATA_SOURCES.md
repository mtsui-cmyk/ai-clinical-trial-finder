# DATA_SOURCES.md

## Source Priority

### Phase 1

- ClinicalTrials.gov API v2

Use for:

- Trial records
- Conditions
- Interventions
- Sponsor
- Phase
- Recruitment status
- Countries and locations
- Last update dates
- Results posted flag

### Phase 2

- WHO ICTRP
- EU CTIS
- PubMed lookup by trial identifier

### Phase 3

- Regulatory public databases
- Device public databases
- Company pipeline pages
- Press releases

## Data Principles

- Keep source URL and registry identifier for every record.
- Do not claim completeness beyond covered sources.
- Show source timestamps and local update timestamps.
- Prefer source-linked facts over AI-derived claims.
- Treat company pipeline pages and press releases as public signals, not verified clinical conclusions.
