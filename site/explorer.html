Read public clinical trial records with more context.

TrialCompass is a public registry reading aid. It helps patients and caregivers understand common registry fields, source limitations, and questions to discuss with a licensed clinician.

Find trials near a location Open glossary
Safety boundary: this tool does not recommend a trial, determine eligibility, interpret personal medical records, or provide medical advice. Always verify details in the official registry.

How to use the finder

1

Search by condition and location

Enter a disease name and a city or postcode. Browser location permission is not required; typed location is enough for the current search.

2

Check nearby listed sites

Results prioritize nearest listed site, distance, site status, trial status, phase, source, and official registry link.

3

Verify before acting

Registry records can be incomplete or stale. Use the official registry and clinician discussion questions before contacting a study site.

What the main fields mean

Status
Recruiting
Public registry says the study is currently recruiting participants. Verify at the official source.
Not yet recruiting
The study may plan to recruit, but has not started recruiting according to the registry record.
Enrolling by invitation
Participants may be selected or invited by the study team; this does not mean a patient is eligible.
Location
Nearest listed site
The closest site in the public record based on available coordinates or source location data.
Site status
Location-level recruiting status can differ from the overall trial status.
Public contact
Contact information copied from the public registry record, not a private referral or endorsement.

What AI can do here

Explain registry language

Turn public fields such as phase, status, condition, intervention, and site information into plainer language.

Surface verification points

Point out what should be checked in the official registry, including site status, contact details, and eligibility text.

Prepare clinician questions

Generate neutral questions a patient may bring to a licensed clinician without deciding eligibility or recommending a trial.

Source limitations

ClinicalTrials.gov is the connected source in this MVP. ANZCTR, WHO ICTRP, EU CTIS, and national registries are shown as planned or external sources until each connector and access model is validated.

Distances are calculated at query time for the current search. TrialCompass does not store patient profiles or raw location history in the runtime cache.
