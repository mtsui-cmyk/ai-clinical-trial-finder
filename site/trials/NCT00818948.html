What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • Lupus nephritis / renal: lupus-related kidney inflammation or kidney monitoring terms.
  • Randomized / placebo / blinded / open-label: study design terms that describe how researchers compare or observe groups.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.
  • PK / PD: terms about how a study drug moves through the body and what biological effects are measured.

Source text

Official summary excerpt

This is a 2-part, multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multiple dose escalation study, enrolling approximately 48 subjects. Part A of the study will enroll subjects with Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) without Glomerulonephritis (GN) into 3 cohorts. Part B of the study will enroll SLE subjects with GN into 3 cohorts. The purpose of the study is to evaluate the multiple dose of AMG 811 on safety. Tolerability and pharmacokinetics.
