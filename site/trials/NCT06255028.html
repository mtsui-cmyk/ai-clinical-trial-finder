What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • Lupus nephritis / renal: lupus-related kidney inflammation or kidney monitoring terms.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.
  • Recruitment status: public registry wording about whether a study is open, opening, invitation-only, or active without recruiting.

Source text

Official summary excerpt

CALiPSO-1 is a Phase 1, multi-centre, dose-confirmation study to evaluate the safety and efficacy of CNTY-101 in participants with refractory B cell-mediated autoimmune diseases including those with moderate to severe systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) with or without lupus nephritis (LN), idiopathic inflammatory myopathies (IIM), and diffuse cutaneous systemic sclerosis (DcSSc).
