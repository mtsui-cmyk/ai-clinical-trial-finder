What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.
  • Randomized / placebo / blinded / open-label: study design terms that describe how researchers compare or observe groups.
  • Phase 2: research that may explore dose, activity, or early effectiveness signals.
  • PK / PD: terms about how a study drug moves through the body and what biological effects are measured.
