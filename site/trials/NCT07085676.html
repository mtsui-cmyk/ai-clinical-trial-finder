Phase 1 Study of HBI0101 CAR-T in Refractory B-Cell Autoimmune Diseases

Recruiting Phase 1 NCT07085676 AI explained

This early-phase study is testing a new treatment called HBI0101 CART (chimeric antigen receptor T-cells) for people with B-cell mediated autoimmune rheumatic diseases. The main goal is to evaluate safety and find the highest dose that can be given safely.

Research context

Study of HBI0101 CAR-T for Autoimmune Diseases

Researchers are studying HBI0101 CART, a type of cellular immunotherapy, to see if it is safe and what dose can be tolerated. The study is for patients with various autoimmune diseases such as lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, and others.

What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • CAR-T / cell therapy: immune-cell therapy being studied in some records; this page does not say it is suitable for anyone.
  • BCMA: an immune-cell target that may appear in some cell therapy or antibody research.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.

Official summary excerpt

A Phase 1 study of HBI0101 BCMA-CART in B-Cell Mediated Autoimmune Rheumatic Diseases. The goal of the study is evaluation of safety and identification of the maximum HBI0101 CART dose that may be administered safely to patients with B-cell mediated autoimmune disease.
