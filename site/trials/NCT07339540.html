What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • BCMA: an immune-cell target that may appear in some cell therapy or antibody research.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.
  • PK / PD: terms about how a study drug moves through the body and what biological effects are measured.
  • Recruitment status: public registry wording about whether a study is open, opening, invitation-only, or active without recruiting.

Official summary excerpt

This study is designed as a single arm, open label, single center clinical trial to evaluate the safety, tolerability, efficacy, pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic characteristics of the investigational drug V001-BCMA in autoimmune disease.
