What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • CAR-T / cell therapy: immune-cell therapy being studied in some records; this page does not say it is suitable for anyone.
  • CD19: an immune-cell marker that some cell therapy and antibody studies target.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.
  • Randomized / placebo / blinded / open-label: study design terms that describe how researchers compare or observe groups.

Source text

Official summary excerpt

This is an open-label, single-dose, prospective, monocenter, Phase I interventional study (not first-in-human) to assess the safety, tolerability, and preliminary efficacy of autologous anti-CD19 CAR-T cells as an advanced therapy medicinal product in adult patients; with severe and refractory systemic autoimmune rheumatic diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis (RA), systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), Sjogren's disease (SjD), systemic sclerosis (SSc) and idiopathic inflammatory myositis (IIM).
