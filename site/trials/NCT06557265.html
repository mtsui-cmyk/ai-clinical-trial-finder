What terms may be confusing?

  • Lupus nephritis / renal: lupus-related kidney inflammation or kidney monitoring terms.
  • CD19: an immune-cell marker that some cell therapy and antibody studies target.
  • Randomized / placebo / blinded / open-label: study design terms that describe how researchers compare or observe groups.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.
  • Phase 2: research that may explore dose, activity, or early effectiveness signals.

Source text

Official summary excerpt

This is an open-label, multi-center, non-randomized, Phase 1/2 study to determine the safety and tolerability of NKX019 (allogeneic CAR NK cells targeting CD19) in participants with active lupus nephritis (LN) or primary membranous nephropathy (pMN).
