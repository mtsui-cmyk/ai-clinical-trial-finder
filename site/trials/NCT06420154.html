The Safety and Efficacy of Anti-CD19 CAR-T Cells in Patients With Relapsed/Refractory Autoimmune Diseases

This public registry record describes Early Phase 1 research involving anti-CD19-CAR-T cells for Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE), Sjogren's Syndrome, Systemic Sclerosis. Its listed recruitment status is not yet recruiting. Listed countries include China. This summary is for research awareness only, not medical advice or trial eligibility guidance.

What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • CAR-T / cell therapy: immune-cell therapy being studied in some records; this page does not say it is suitable for anyone.
  • CD19: an immune-cell marker that some cell therapy and antibody studies target.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.
