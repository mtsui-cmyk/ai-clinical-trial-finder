A Clinical Study of MK-1045 in People With Lupus or Rheumatoid Arthritis (MK-1045-004)

Recruiting Phase 1 NCT07363590 AI explained

This study looks at a study medicine called MK-1045 in people with lupus and rheumatoid arthritis (RA). The main goal of the study is to learn about the safety of MK-1045 and if people tolerate it when they receive it at different dose levels (amounts).

Research context

Study of MK-1045 for Lupus or Rheumatoid Arthritis

Researchers are studying the safety and tolerability of different doses of MK-1045 in people with lupus or rheumatoid arthritis.

AI-explained record: This record has a reviewed source-grounded AI explanation cache. It is a reading aid only and is not medically reviewed.

What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.
  • Phase 1: early research that often focuses on safety, dose, or tolerability questions.
  • Recruitment status: public registry wording about whether a study is open, opening, invitation-only, or active without recruiting.
