What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
  • Lupus nephritis / renal: lupus-related kidney inflammation or kidney monitoring terms.
  • CAR-T / cell therapy: immune-cell therapy being studied in some records; this page does not say it is suitable for anyone.
  • CD19: an immune-cell marker that some cell therapy and antibody studies target.
  • Biologic / antibody: an immune-targeting research intervention category.

Source text

Official summary excerpt

Primary objective: Safety and tolerability of NKX019, administered after lymphodepletion (LD). Secondary objectives: * Assess clinical activity of NKX019 in subjects with systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) with or without active lupus nephritis (LN) * Characterize pharmacokinetics (PK) of NKX019 * Characterize immunogenicity of NKX019
