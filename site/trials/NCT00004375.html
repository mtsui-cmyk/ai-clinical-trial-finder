Ultraviolet Light Therapy for Systemic Lupus Erythematosus

Unknown Phase 2 NCT00004375 AI explained

This study looks at how ultraviolet A-1 (UVA-1) light therapy may affect people with systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) and healthy volunteers. Researchers want to understand the mechanisms of this therapy.

Research context

Ultraviolet A-1 Light Therapy for Lupus

The researchers are studying the effects of ultraviolet A-1 light treatment on the immune system and symptoms in people with lupus and in healthy individuals.

AI-explained record: This record has a reviewed source-grounded AI explanation cache. It is a reading aid only and is not medically reviewed.

What terms may be confusing?

  • SLE: systemic lupus erythematosus, the medical name for lupus in many registry records.
